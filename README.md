# Bachelor's in Computer Science

Coursework from my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science at the University of Trento (2021–2024): lecture notes, exercises, assignments, projects and the final thesis, organised by year and semester.

Documents are provided as PDF so they can be read directly on GitHub. Most lecture notes are written in **Italian**; the English-taught courses (Computer and Network Security, Networking, Machine Learning) are in English. The notes are my own and include figures taken from the course slides.
